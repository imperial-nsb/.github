# Noninvasive Surgery & Biopsy Laboratory

**Welcome to the Noninvasive Surgery & Biopsy Laboratory at Imperial College London.**

## 📖 About Us

The NSBLab is a multidisciplinary research group located in the Department of Bioengineering at Imperial College London. Led by **Dr. James Choi**, our team comprises engineers, physicists, chemists, and biologists working together to develop next-generation medical technologies.

Our primary goal is to build devices that can manipulate and sense tissue from the surface, enabling safe, consistent, and complex procedures without invasive surgery.

## 🔬 Research

We focus on two main research areas: **Noninvasive Microsurgery for Brain Drug Delivery** and **Wearable Controllers**.

### 1. Noninvasive Microsurgery for Brain Drug Delivery

Neurological diseases are often untreatable because the Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB) prevents drugs from entering the brain. We are developing technologies to overcome this challenge using **focused ultrasound and microbubbles**.

* **Mechanism**: Microbubbles are co-administered with drugs. Acoustic wavelets (short, low-pressure ultrasound pulses) drive these bubbles to oscillate, temporarily opening the BBB to allow drug passage.
* **Monitoring**: We use the acoustic emissions from the bubbles to monitor the procedure in real-time, ensuring safety and precision.
* **Disease Targets**:
* **Alzheimer's Disease** (in collaboration with Prof. Magdalena Sastre, Dr. Francesco Aprile, Prof. Nicholas Long)
* **Glioblastoma Multiforme** (in collaboration with Dr. Nelofer Syed)
* **Diffuse Intrinsic Pontine Glioma (DIPG)** (in collaboration with Prof. Chris Jones, Prof. Simon Robinson, Prof. Gail ter Haar)



### 2. Wearable Controllers for VR/AR

We are developing novel wearable technologies capable of tracking body movements (hands, feet, and face) for immersive Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality applications.

### 🧪 Lab Facilities & Equipment

Our lab is equipped to run experiments across multiple levels, from physics and phantoms to *in vitro* and *in vivo* studies.

**Key Equipment:**

* Ultra high-speed video camera (20 million frames per second)
* Custom ultrasound emitters and receivers (single-element, linear arrays, 256-element therapeutic arrays)
* Microbubble manufacturing setup
* Optical microscopes with high-frame-rate cameras
* 3D Printing facilities (FormLabs 3+)

**Departmental & Collaborator Access:**

* Histology & Tissue Culture Rooms
* Microscopy Suite (confocal, 2-photon)
* Electronics & Machine Workshops
* 9Tesla MRI Scanners & Verasonics imaging engines
